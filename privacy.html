Privacy

FishyTweaks is built to work on your PC, not on a server. This page lists every place the app and this website connect to, and why.

What stays on your PC

  • Profiles. Your FishyTweaks profile is local. Passwords are stored as argon2 hashes and never leave the PC.
  • Measurements. FPS, sessions, hardware scans and FPS Doctor results are saved locally. A result only leaves your PC if you share it yourself.
  • Undo records. The previous values that let FishyTweaks undo changes are stored next to the app's settings.
  • Error log. Errors are written to a local file. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • AI keys. If you add an API key for Fishy AI, it's encrypted with Windows' own data protection and stored on your PC.

What the app sends

  • An anonymous usage count. Once when installed, and once per day while used, the app adds +1 to a public counter at abacus.jasoncameron.dev. No name, account, hardware details or ID is sent. Like any web request, the counter service sees your IP address; we never receive it.
  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub for the latest release and downloads updates from GitHub Releases. Updates are signature-checked before they install.
  • This website's notice files. The app reads broadcast.json (announcements) and links.json (the Discord link) from this site.
  • Fortnite item shop. The shop card loads today's shop from fortnite-api.com. Nothing about you is sent.

Optional features that use other services

  • Fishy AI with your own key. Your messages go to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) and are handled under that provider's terms.
  • Fishy AI on your GPU. The local model is downloaded once from Hugging Face; chats then stay on your PC.
  • Free picture maker. If you use it, the picture description is sent to pollinations.ai.

What it never does

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • No reading of the Fortnite process or its memory.
  • No online account is required.

This website

  • No cookies and no analytics.
  • Your language choice is saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the download button asks GitHub's API for the latest version. Both services see your IP address, as with any website.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages.
